Direction A · Clean & Minimal

A quiet, editorial page that reads like a well-made product site. Serif headlines set the tone; everything else is plain text on generous whitespace, so the project imagery carries the visual weight. There are no cards, no chips and almost no colour: one cobalt accent marks the next action on every screen.

Serves recruiters and hiring managers best. It reads top to bottom in one pass, scans well in 30 seconds, ages slowly, and is the cheapest of the three to build and maintain.

Typography

Newsreader is a text serif built for screens, with optical sizes, so large headlines stay crisp and calm without looking decorative. Public Sans is a neutral, very legible grotesque with open shapes, less worn than Inter. IBM Plex Mono is used only for metadata and code; it signals engineering without a terminal theme.

Motion

Almost still. Motion only confirms that something is interactive.

  • Page load. Hero headline, intro and actions fade in once (opacity 0→1, 8px rise, 500ms, 60ms stagger). Nothing else animates on scroll.
  • Project media. On hover the frame lifts 4px over 400ms, cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1). The screenshot does not zoom.
  • Links and buttons. Colour and underline changes over 150ms ease-out. Buttons press down 1px on active.
  • Header. Stays 64px; its bottom border appears after 8px of scroll.
  • Theme switch. Background and text colours cross-fade over 200ms. The theme is set before first paint so there is no flash.
  • Mobile menu. Sheet fades in over 200ms; links do not stagger.
  • Reduced motion. All transforms are removed; only colour and opacity changes remain, at 100ms or less.
